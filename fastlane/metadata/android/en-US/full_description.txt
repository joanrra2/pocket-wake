Pocket Wake turns on the computers at home from wherever you are, with one tap.

It works with a Pocket AdBlock board on your home network. The phone sends the request to an MQTT broker of your choice (for example a free EMQX Cloud Serverless broker); the board picks it up, sends the Wake-on-LAN magic packet on your network, checks that the PC answers, and the app shows "Online after 23 s".

What you need
• A Pocket AdBlock board connected to your home network, with Remote wake (MQTT) turned on.
• An MQTT broker with TLS, and a login for the phone.
• A PC with Wake-on-LAN turned on (wired Ethernet).
The setup guide walks through all of it: https://github.com/joanrra2/pocket-wake/blob/main/docs/SETUP.md

What it shows
• Connection status, and why it can't connect: wrong password, untrusted certificate, wrong port.
• Whether the board is online.
• One card per PC with its online dot. PCs that have an IP on the board appear by themselves; others can be added by name.
• Wake progress and the result: "Online after 41 s", "Already online", or a hint when nothing answers. With board firmware 0.6 or later, the board's own wait per PC, a Cancel button, and wakes started from Home Assistant or another phone.
• Watch-only devices (a NAS, a printer): online dot, no Wake button.
• When each PC was last woken.
• The board's blocking switch, statistics, uptime and last restart, if your broker login is allowed to read them.

Quick to use
• Scan the QR code on the board's setup page to fill in the broker address, port and topic prefix.
• Test connection checks the login, TLS, the board and the broker's access rules before you save.
• Long-press the app icon for the last woken PCs, or add a PC's own icon to the home screen.
• Tasker and NFC tags can ask the app to wake a PC; it always asks you to confirm.

Private by design
• No accounts, analytics, ads or trackers. Nothing goes to the developer.
• The app talks only to the broker you enter, over TLS (on by default), verified against Android's trusted certificate authorities.
• The broker password is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. App backups are off.
• The camera is used only when you tap Scan; frames are decoded on the phone.
• No background service: the app connects only while it is open.
